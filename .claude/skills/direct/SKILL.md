name: direct
description: Full creative-director pass on new material in inbox/ (footage, headshot, or audio). Use for /direct, "edit this", "what should I make", or when files are dropped without instructions.
---
1. Inventory `inbox/`: probe each file (ffprobe), transcribe speech, and look at 6 stills.
2. Pitch 3 concepts as a table: Hook | Treatment | Effect stack | Apps needed | Effort. At least one must be "almost impossible" (e.g. drone pull-back from a desk shot, the creator splitting into 3 angles at once, the creator stepping out of a phone screen).
3. On approval: run the local steps (cutout, captions, Remotion effects, ffmpeg), and write `projects/<slug>/prompts.md` for every cloud step with exact app + settings.
4. Finish with a checklist: ✅ done by Claude / 👤 creator to do / 🔁 bring back to inbox.
