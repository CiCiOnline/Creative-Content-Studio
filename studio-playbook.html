SetupFundamentals, in order

  1. 1

    Make the studio permanent

    Today's studio lives in a cloud workspace that is wiped when idle. Create a private GitHub repo (e.g. studio), connect GitHub to Claude, and send me the name. I'll push everything and create a saved environment that reinstalls the tools on every session (setup.sh is ready).

  2. 2

    Shoot your clone kit (one afternoon)

    • Headshots: 5 to 10 photos, front-facing, eye level, soft window light, mouth closed and slightly open, no sunglasses or hands near the face. Highest resolution your phone allows.
    • Avatar footage: 2 to 3 minutes talking to camera, still head, natural gestures, 4K 30fps, plain background.
    • Voice: 3 to 5 minutes clean audio in a quiet, soft room (closet or duvet), lav or USB mic, mixed energy (calm, excited, questioning). ElevenLabs Professional clones improve up to about 30 min.
    • Drop them in the session; I'll file them under assets/faces and assets/voice.
  3. 3

    Open the four accounts

    • HeyGen: create a Photo Avatar or Video Avatar and connect your ElevenLabs voice.
    • ElevenLabs: Voice Lab, then Professional Voice Clone, and verify.
    • Higgsfield: covers Seedance 2.5 and Kling in one place, for angles and motion control.
    • Runway: Aleph, for edits by typing. Start every app on its cheapest paid tier and upgrade only when credits run out.
  4. 4

    Lock your look

    Pick 2 fonts, 4 colours, a caption style and an intro move. Send references you love and I'll save them in CLAUDE.md so every edit stays recognisably yours.

  5. 5

    Film for AI, not just for camera

    • 9:16, 4K, 30fps, phone at eye level, key light 45° to one side.
    • Plain background a few feet behind you: cleaner cutouts and easier scene swaps.
    • 1 second of stillness at the start and end of each take. Generators lock onto it.
    • Shoot one wide take. Seedance can generate the close-ups and drone shots from it.
  6. 6

    Run your first video

    Send footage or a headshot plus a topic and type /direct. You get 3 concepts, the local edit, and a prompts file for each app step.
